### Layer 1 — Participant profile

Use structured/MCQ responses for these:

1. Current role
2. Years of relevant experience
3. Industry / sector
4. Type of digital product or platform
5. Frequency of using data/analytics in product decisions
6. Frequency of using AI-based tools in product-related work

### Layer 2 — Core questions

1. **Decision process**

   Think of a recent product decision you were involved in. How did the decision develop from the initial problem or signal to the final action?

1. **Current data, tools and evidence**

   What types of data, tools, processes, or other evidence do you currently use when making product decisions, and how do they influence the decision?

1. **Current AI use**

   Where, if anywhere, do AI-based tools currently support your product decision-making?

1. **Data quality and reliability**

   How do you determine whether the available data is reliable and sufficient enough to support a decision?

1. **Data-to-decision problems and consequences**

   What are the main difficulties in moving from data or analytical insights to an actual product decision, and what consequences do these difficulties have?

1. **AI trust and validation**

   When AI produces an insight, explanation, or recommendation, how do you decide whether to trust, verify, or challenge it?

1. **Prioritisation and trade-offs**

   When several possible actions or opportunities exist, how are they compared and prioritised?

1. **Governance, responsibility and risk**

   What organisational, governance, privacy, security, compliance, or responsibility constraints affect how data and AI can be used in product decisions?

1. **Outcome measurement and learning**

   After a decision is implemented, how do you assess whether it produced the expected result, and how is that learning used in later decisions?

1. **Requirements for better decision support**

   What would a useful decision-support process need to provide to help you make better-supported product decisions using data and AI?

### Optional probes for call interviews

Use only when needed:

- Can you give a concrete example?
- Why did that happen?
- Who was involved?
- Who had final responsibility?
- What happened when people disagreed?
- What was missing?
- What would you change?
- What happens when the data is uncertain or conflicting?
