# Interview Guide V1

Thesis title: From Data to Product Decisions: Designing an AI-Assisted Product Analytics Framework for Digital Platforms

Interview type: Semi-structured expert interview

Target participants:

- Product managers
- Product owners
- Product analysts
- Growth/product operations managers
- UX researchers working with product data
- Data analysts supporting product teams
- AI or analytics practitioners involved in product decisions

Estimated duration: 30-45 minutes

## Interview Goal

The goal of the interview is to understand how product professionals currently use product data and AI-based tools in decision-making, what challenges they face, and what requirements an AI-assisted product analytics framework should fulfill.

## Opening Script

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. I am writing my master thesis on AI-assisted product analytics and product decision-making in digital platforms.

The purpose of this interview is to understand your practical experience with product data, analytics tools, and AI-based decision-support tools. There are no right or wrong answers. I am interested in your real workflow, challenges, and expectations.

With your permission, I would like to take notes and use anonymized insights from this interview in my thesis. Your name and company will not be mentioned unless you explicitly allow it.

## Section 1: Role and Context

1. Can you briefly describe your current role and your involvement in product decisions?

2. What type of digital product, platform, or service do you work with?

3. What kinds of product decisions are you usually involved in?
   - Feature prioritization
   - Roadmap planning
   - User experience improvement
   - Experimentation or A/B testing
   - Growth or retention decisions
   - Operational product decisions

## Section 2: Current Use of Product Data

4. What types of data do you usually use when making product decisions?

5. Which analytics or data tools do you currently use?

6. Can you describe a recent product decision where data played an important role?

7. How do you usually move from data or insight to an actual product decision?

8. Where does this process work well, and where does it become difficult?

## Section 3: Current Use of AI-Based Tools

9. Do you currently use AI tools in your product management or analytics workflow?

10. If yes, what do you use them for?

- Summarizing user feedback
- Analyzing product data
- Generating hypotheses
- Prioritizing ideas
- Writing requirements or tickets
- Supporting roadmap decisions
- Explaining trends or anomalies

11. Can you describe one example where an AI tool helped you make or prepare a product decision?

12. What do AI tools currently do well in your workflow?

13. What do AI tools currently fail to do well?

## Section 4: Challenges and Limitations

14. What are the biggest challenges when translating product data into actionable decisions?

15. What makes it difficult to trust data-based or AI-generated insights?

16. Are there situations where data or AI creates confusion rather than clarity?

17. What human judgment is still necessary even when data or AI is available?

18. What organizational or workflow barriers affect the use of data and AI in product decisions?

## Section 5: Requirements for an AI-Assisted Framework

19. If you could design an ideal AI-assisted product analytics workflow, what should it help you with?

20. What information should such a framework include before recommending or supporting a decision?

21. What would make the framework useful for product managers in daily work?

22. What would make the framework trustworthy?

23. What should the framework avoid doing?

24. How should AI-generated insights be connected to prioritization, roadmap, or feature decisions?

## Section 6: Evaluation and Closing

25. If I develop a framework for AI-assisted product decision-making, what criteria should I use to evaluate whether it is useful?

26. Would you be willing to review the final framework later and give feedback?

27. Is there anything important about data, AI, or product decision-making that we did not discuss?

## Optional Follow-Up Prompts

- Can you give me a concrete example?
- What happened after that decision?
- Who was involved in the decision?
- What data was missing?
- How did you decide whether to trust the insight?
- What would have made the process easier?
- Was the AI output directly used, or did someone reinterpret it?
